# Sign-in problems

## "Invalid credentials"

The email or password is wrong, or the account is locked. Mentis shows the same message in both cases on purpose.

## Locked account

After 5 failed attempts the account is locked for 15 minutes. Wait, or ask a super-admin to unlock it.

"Too many attempts. Wait a minute and try again." means too many sign-in requests came from your network address in the last minute: wait a minute and retry.

## Lost or new phone

If you lost the authenticator app, ask a super-admin to **reset your 2FA**. At your next sign-in you will scan a new QR code.

## "Login step expired"

The time between password and code is limited to 5 minutes. Start again from the sign-in page.
