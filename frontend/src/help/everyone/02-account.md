# Your account

Open **Account** by selecting your name at the bottom of the left menu. On a phone, open the menu first with the ☰ button at the top left.

## Profile

Select **Edit** to change your display name. Email and role are set by an admin.

## Appearance

Choose **Light**, **Medium** or **Dark**. The theme applies at once and follows you on every device.

## Password

Enter your current password, then the new one twice, and select **Update password**. Other sessions are signed out; this browser stays signed in. Five wrong current passwords lock the account for 15 minutes and sign you out, like failed sign-ins.

If your session expires or is ended elsewhere, the next action takes you back to the sign-in page.

## Sign out

Use **Sign out** at the top of the Account page.
