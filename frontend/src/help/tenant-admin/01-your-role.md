# Your role as tenant admin

You administer the customers ticked on your account (or all of them, if you have **All customers**): their documentation, settings, LLM and users. You do not see the other customers of the platform.

## Several customers

If you administer more than one customer, the **customer selector** at the top of the menu chooses which one Documents, LLM, Users and Settings show. Your choice is remembered on this device. On a phone, open the menu with the ☰ button.

## What you can do today

- **Documents**: upload procedures, escalation matrices and runbooks (PDF, Word, Excel, PowerPoint), manage versions and check what Mentis read in the preview, and test questions against it.
- **Users**: invite and manage tenant admins and analysts of your customers.
- **Settings**: decide how answers use your documentation.
- **LLM**: choose which AI model answers for your customer, and a fallback (when the super-admin allows it).
- **General**: if you have General, pick it in the customer selector to manage the documentation shared by all customers.
- **Chat**: test the answers analysts get about your customers. `@` lists your customers; **All my customers** searches all of them.

Analysts ask questions about your customer in the chat and get answers with citations from the documents in use. Keep them current: an outdated contact in a document is an outdated answer.
