# Your role as tenant admin

You administer one customer. You manage its documentation, its settings and its other admins. You do not see other customers.

## What you can do today

- **Documents**: upload procedures, escalation matrices and runbooks (PDF, Word, Excel, PowerPoint), manage versions and check what Mentis read in the preview.
- **Users**: invite and manage the admins of your customer.
- **Settings**: decide how answers use your documentation.

## Coming next

- **Answers from your documents**: indexing and the "test a question" box in the preview.
- **Integrations**: read-only connections to ticketing, EDR and SIEM tools.
- **LLM**: choose which AI model answers for your customer.
