# Users of your customer

## Invite another admin

1. Open **Users** and select **Invite user**.
2. Enter name and email, then select **Send invitation**.

The new user becomes a tenant admin of your customer. If email is not configured, Mentis shows the link to send yourself. Links work once and expire after 72 hours.

## Manage

- Pencil icon: change the name or turn the account off.
- Trash icon: delete the user.

Analysts and super-admins are managed by the platform super-admin. For a lost phone (2FA reset), contact a super-admin.
