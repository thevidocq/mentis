# Your role as analyst

You consult Mentis to find what each customer's procedures say: escalations, contacts, SLAs, runbooks. Mentis only reads; it never changes anything in customer systems.

- You can ask about **every customer**, not only some.
- Your chats are **private**: other users, admins included, cannot read them.
- The **Chat** page opens right after you sign in.

See **Using the chat** for how to ask and how to read the answers.
